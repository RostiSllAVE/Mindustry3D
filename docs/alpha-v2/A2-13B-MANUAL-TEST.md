# A2-13b — Owner test

Keep A2-13 and a backed-up/disposable save. Import `local/a2-13b/ready/mindustry-fpv-a2-13b-diagnostics.jar` through Mods → Import Mod, confirm replacement and restart. Use Fullscreen. For multiplayer, every client and server must use this exact JAR; this is an unpublished diagnostic.

1. **Appearance and immersion:** Inspect shallow/deep water and shores from low/overhead FPV. The bed should be deeper than the separate moving surface. Check sun glints, oil/lava bubbles over a full cycle, and cryofluid ice appearing, gathering/drifting and melting. Try Settings → FPV flight → Animated 3D liquid waves OFF/ON. In a native flying unit, use Stabilized Q/E or Acro to descend into water and rise out: check coloured immersion, fading tint and then disappearing droplets. Try oil/cryo and, briefly on a disposable invulnerable test setup if desired, slag. Hard bed impacts and lava statuses remain real hazards.
2. **Gameplay and AI:** Compare a one-cell lava puddle with a large connected lake. Approach sideways and from above: burning should start farther away than melting, with a taller upward volume. Water/oil/cryo should apply their native contact status. Watch idle flying AI climb above effect volumes; then give attack/repair/mining work and check it retains its normal approach. Sail small/large boats and check their ordinary movement/level remains intact. Walls/roofs do not provide heat insulation in this proximity model.
3. **Regressions and platforms:** Use Tab, F3 pause/resume, F5 and F7, change units, select blueprint priority and place/remove blocks on Z0/Z2. Check water beneath platforms, ore/mining and save/reload/campaign transitions. Verify no stuck input after immersion. Repeat on Linux if available and compare the same map/settings FPS, especially large oceans and many units. Report visual artifacts, unexpected status ranges, AI stalls or extra stutter.

The shader samples the camera lens: an external camera above the lake stays above-water even if the unit is submerged. Diving is enabled only for player-controlled native flyers; ground units/boats/ground creator flight keep previous physical support. There is no new underwater ballistics, fluid drag, buoyancy or oxygen system. Film is cosmetic and uses render time; native statuses use simulation/server time.

Automated/native fixtures and scripted graphics are documented separately in the report. Physical play, Linux and independent two-computer acceptance remain the owner's tests. Do not publish this diagnostic or update Workshop automatically.
