# A2-11 — Owner visual acceptance

Use a disposable profile or backup copy of a save, Mindustry 160.7, and **Fullscreen**. Preserve the previous A2-10 JAR as a rollback candidate. Import `local/a2-11/test-kit/mindustry-fpv-a2-11-diagnostics.jar` through Mods → Import Mod, replace the previous test candidate and restart. No extra Java installation, command line, assets or settings are required. This is an unreleased diagnostic; the normal metadata version has not been bumped.

1. **Inspect surfaces.** On a map containing stone/soil, sand/snow, ice, ores, water and mountains, press F5 and look along the ground at a low angle, then from above. Expect gentle irregularities rather than new hills, spikes or tile steps. Walk/fly across several 16-tile chunk boundaries and move near/far: there should be no holes or cracks. Relief fades flat at chunk edges and in the distant tier. Check water/shore boundaries, metal floors, trees, bushes, rocks and copper/lead ore. Water/artificial floors and prop bases should remain flat; ore should follow the natural floor texture. Report any visible grid-shaped apron or texture seam.

2. **Check structures and controls.** Near a mountain and an artificial wall, enter Tab Build Mode. Place/remove a small block, a multi-tile factory, and an elevated platform with blocks on Z2. Check that foundations do not float/sink and nearby ground refreshes after removal. Verify selection and multi-floor copy/paste, Alt-click blueprint priority, F3 pause/resume, F5/Tab transitions and normal FPV mouse capture. No floor, support, collision or build coordinate should change; floors can differ visually from physical Z0 by at most 0.065 tile.

3. **Reload and compare performance.** Save and reload the disposable map: visible relief must be identical. Compare A2-10 and A2-11 with the same save, resolution, fullscreen/VSync/FPS settings and camera angles, including low-angle ground views and vertical views. Record FPS/frame time and any stutters after chunks have loaded as well as during movement. Repeat on Linux if available. For a multiplayer check all peers/server need the same diagnostic payload; mixed A2-10/A2-11 payloads must still be rejected. A real two-computer check is optional and remains unverified until performed.

No new bindings are introduced. Please report platform, map/floor type, viewing angle, FPS before/after and screenshots for any cracks, excessive relief, prop/building alignment or texture problems. Automated CPU tests do not replace this physical visual acceptance.
