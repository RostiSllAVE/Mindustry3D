# A2-16o polish — three owner checks

Use the new `local/a2-16o-polish/test-kit/mindustry-fpv-a2-16o-polish-diagnostics.jar` through normal Mods → Import Mod → replace → restart. Keep one Mindustry³ version installed. Fullscreen is recommended for the previously documented windowed presentation issue. A disposable map/save is sufficient.

1. **Repairs:** place Repair Point and Repair Turret at Z0 and on Z1/Z3/Z5 platforms. Bring a damaged friendly unit into actual healing range. Inspect from above and low FPV: complete native head, seated base, independent rotation, beam from its normal point, HP increasing. Move the target away; healing must stop normally. Save/reload and recheck.
2. **Turrets:** compare Duo with Arc, Lancer, Meltdown, Wave, Tsunami, Hail and Ripple, plus Parallax/Segment. Inspect from top, side and oblique angles; rotate/fire at real enemies where appropriate. Check muzzle/recoil alignment, fluid/heat overlays and the unchanged Salvo wings/Cyclone parts. Repeat a few examples on platforms and in a dense installation.
3. **Aircraft:** use Flare, Zenith and a multi-engine Quell/Disrupt, then several aircraft together. Hover, accelerate, bank/roll, zoom and switch FPV/rear/external views; check jets stay on engines and pulse. Your hidden FPV body must not leave a jet across the screen; other aircraft should retain jets. Fly behind a solid structure and remove/kill a test aircraft to check normal depth and immediate cleanup. Verify F5, Tab and menu cursor release still behave normally.

Please report the block/unit name, view, floor and a screenshot for any clipping or detached effect. Actual Linux and two-computer gameplay remain separate acceptance tests; automated Windows renders do not certify those environments.
