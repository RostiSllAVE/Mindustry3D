# A2-16i — Three owner checks

Import `local/a2-16i/test-kit-final/mindustry-fpv-a2-16i-diagnostics.jar` through
Mindustry160.7 **Mods → Import Mod**, replace the local test build and restart.
Use a disposable map or backed-up save; Windows fullscreen is recommended. Source
ZIP is not the mod. One portable JAR is supplied for Windows/Linux.

1. **Repair, boost and build.** Place Mender, Mend Projector, Regen Projector,
   Overdrive Projector/Dome and Build Tower. Supply their native resources/power
   and create real repair/build work. Compare working, no-target, disabled and
   F3-paused states from above and low FPV. Mend lights mean readiness; Regen must
   not claim active repair from warmup alone. Booster indicators must follow real
   optional input. No new outward area or functional drone should appear.
2. **Shield and storage.** Check Force Projector active, hit, depleted and recovery
   states near an opaque wall and overlapping shields. Protection must retain its
   existing finite XYZ boundaries. Compare Container, Vault and both reinforced
   variants empty/half/full, also linked to a core. Indicators show the most-filled
   actual item slot, not total warehouse capacity. Check loading arches and roofs.
3. **Floors and regression.** With ordinary support/headroom, place on Z1/Z3/Z5,
   copy/paste, save/reload and change sector. Copies must not duplicate inventory.
   Try selection/ghosts, Alt-click priority, F3, F5 and Tab. Inspect below platforms
   and dense support/storage factories; report FPS/runtime/platform. Confirm the
   previously accepted liquid/power models and XYZ connections remain correct.

No controls, costs, influence ranges or gameplay rules are added. Normal campaign
unlock/environment requirements remain native. Editor-only Erekir BaseShield
models are deferred. Use identical JARs for multiplayer. Physical Linux, remote
multiplayer and owner visual/input acceptance remain pending.
